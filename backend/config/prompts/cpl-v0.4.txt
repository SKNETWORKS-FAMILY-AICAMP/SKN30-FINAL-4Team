PURPOSE_GOAL axes:
- PURPOSE_TARGET_CONDITION: 대상 한정 조건. wording that explicitly limits the
  supported target set. 경쟁력 강화, 디지털 전환, 시장진출, and 기술사업화 alone
  are not target conditions.
- PURPOSE_PROBLEM_DOMAIN: 문제·지원 영역. the source wording that expresses the
  problem or support domain. Do not convert it to a closed taxonomy label.
- PURPOSE_SPECIFIC_OBJECTIVE: 구체적 목표. the concrete objective being pursued,
  such as source wording about market entry, commercialization, productivity, or
  financing.
- PURPOSE_DIRECTION: 변화 방향. explicit change or direction wording such as
  expansion, promotion, strengthening, improvement, advancement, or resolution.
  Tag both objective and direction only when the source explicitly states both.
  Do not infer a direction from an objective alone.

TARGET_AND_CONDITIONS axes:
- TARGET_GROUP: 대상군. 중소기업, 소상공인, 창업기업, 예비창업자 등
- COND_COMPANY_TYPE: 기업유형. 법인, 개인사업자, 중견기업, 컨소시엄 등
- COND_INDUSTRY: 업종·산업. ICT, 제조, 바이오, 식품 등
- COND_REGION: 지역. 소재지 제한
- COND_BUSINESS_AGE: 업력·성장단계. 창업 7년 이내, 도약기 등
- COND_REVENUE: 매출 기준
- COND_HEADCOUNT: 종사자수 기준
- COND_CERTIFICATION: 인증·자격·명시적 상태. 벤처인증, 이노비즈, 법령상 지정상태 등
- COND_OTHER: 위에 속하지 않는 자격 요건. M&A 이력, 제휴 계획 등
- COND_EXCLUSION: 제외 조건. 중복수혜 제한, 참여제한 등

A target sentence that names both who is eligible and what they must satisfy
carries TARGET_GROUP and one or more COND_* axes. Tag each condition separately
with the axis that matches it.
